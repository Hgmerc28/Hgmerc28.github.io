01 / TEST

Flywheel motor test rig

HEAT Robotics, RoboStrike · Fall 2026

Our launcher throws projectiles with two brushless motors spinning flywheels. Before the team designed anything around those motors, I wanted real numbers: when they start, how much current they pull and how warm they get.

  • Built an Arduino rig that drives the ESC with PWM and takes throttle commands over serial.
  • Ran it on a current-limited bench supply, with a 25% throttle cap in the code as a safety limit.
  • Handled ESC arming and throttle calibration.
  • Found the start threshold, measured current and temperature, and logged the results for the launcher team.

02 / SYSTEM DESIGN

RoboStrike electrical system

HEAT Robotics · Lead Electrical Director · 2026 to now

RoboStrike is HEAT Robotics' RoboMaster competition robot. I lead the electrical side: four subteams and about seven people covering drive, launcher, power distribution, and comms and sensors.

  • Designed the prototype electrical architecture: 6S power distribution with a 60 A main fuse, a fuse on every branch and a protected feed for the logic.
  • Planned the STM32F407 interfaces: SBUS, servo bus, I²C power monitoring and CAN.
  • Set one rule for the whole team. Every board and harness passes a current-limited bench test before it touches the power bus.
  • Write the onboarding and training material so new members can wire and test safely.
03 / POWER AND DRIVES

Power and motor drives for a wildfire robot

HEAT Robotics, EMBR · Interim Electrical Lead · 2025 to 2026

EMBR is a tracked robot for the cleanup stage after a wildfire. It drives over burned ground and checks for hotspots that are still holding heat. I worked on the battery, the power harness and the motor drives. The sensing and autonomy are the software team's work.

  • Built the 10S4P Li-ion pack from 18650 cells, with a BMS and a precharge circuit.
  • Built the harness that steps 42 V down to regulated 12 V and 5 V rails.
  • Brought up the maxon ESCON2 drives for the drivetrain and the ODrive S1 controllers for the arm: PWM command mapping, current limits and closed-loop control with AS5047P encoders.

We presented EMBR at the MassRobotics Form & Function Challenge in Boston in May 2026.

04 / PCB DESIGN

Femtoamp measurement board

Chaaban Lab, UBC Okanagan · Research Assistant · July 2026 to now

A femtoamp is a millionth of a billionth of an amp. At that level the circuit board itself can leak more current than the signal you are trying to measure, so the layout matters as much as the schematic.

  • Designed a 4-layer test PCB in Altium around the ADA4530-1, with a guard ring and guard plane protecting the input, followed by filter and gain stages.
  • Simulated the amplifier and filter stages in LTspice before layout.
  • Went through the datasheets pin by pin before ordering and caught a pinout error that would have made the board unusable.
05 / SENSING AND SAFETY

CNC force telemetry and e-stop

UBC CMPE 246 capstone · Feb to Apr 2026 · team of 5

A system that measures cutting forces on a CNC machine in real time and stops the machine when something goes wrong.

  • Half-bridge strain gauge into an instrumentation amp and an ADC.
  • Signal processing in three steps: a median filter, a dual EMA and a rate-of-change gate. It reacts fast to a real event and ignores noise.
  • Coordinated the 5-person team over 11 weeks.
